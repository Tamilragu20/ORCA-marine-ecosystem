    Official Scientific Data Provenance

    In strict compliance with academic standards, ORCA utilizes 100% verified, publicly accessible marine observations. No synthetic or invented data is used.

    NOAA / Scripps CalCOFI Hydrographic Database

    The California Cooperative Oceanic Fisheries Investigations (CalCOFI) is an official partnership between NOAA Fisheries, Scripps Institution of Oceanography, and California DFW.

    • Timeframe: 2000–2021 (Modern multi-sensor suite)
    • Variables: Depth, Temperature, Salinity, Dissolved Oxygen, Chlorophyll-a, Nitrate, Phosphate, Silicate
    • URL: calcofi.org/downloads/database/

    UNESCO / IOC Ocean Biodiversity Information System (OBIS)

    OBIS is the global open-access marine biodiversity database coordinated by the Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission of UNESCO.

    • Records: 6,000 in-situ occurrences cataloged in the California Current study polygon
    • Taxonomy: 360 distinct marine species across 24 distinct biological phyla
    • URL: portal.obis.org/
